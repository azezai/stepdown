# Stepdown

A small, no-signup web app for cutting down on cigarettes. Set a daily limit, log each cigarette with one tap, and see how much money you're saving.

## Features

- **Daily counter.** Shows how many you've had today against your limit.
- **Optional step-down plan.** Start higher and lower the limit by one every few days until you reach your target.
- **Craving timer.** A 5-minute delay with small things to do while the urge passes.
- **Last 7 days.** A bar chart of your daily count against your limit.
- **Money saved.** Compared with what you used to smoke, based on your pack price.

## How to use it

1. Open the site on your phone.
2. Open **Change plan** and set:
   - **Before (per day):** how many you used to smoke
   - **Start per day** and **Target per day:** set both to the same number for a fixed limit
   - **Pack price:** what you pay for a pack of 20
3. Tap **Log a cigarette** each time you smoke.

On iPhone, add it to your home screen in Safari (Share → **Add to Home Screen**) so it opens like an app.

## Privacy

There are no accounts, no tracking, and no server.

Everything you enter is saved only in your own browser on your own device. Nobody else who opens this site can see or change your data, and each visitor gets their own blank copy.

Because data stays on your device, it doesn't sync between your devices. Clearing your browser's website data will erase your log.

## Run your own copy

The whole app is a single file, `index.html`. To host your own copy, fork this repository and turn on GitHub Pages (**Settings → Pages → Deploy from a branch → main**). You can also just open the file in a browser.

## Note

This is a personal habit tracker, not medical advice. If you want help cutting down or quitting, a doctor, a pharmacist, or your local quit-smoking service can help, for example with nicotine replacement.
